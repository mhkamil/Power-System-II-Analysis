## What this shows
1. **Repeats are heavy.** F24b reused Murray loop, string efficiency, 3.5 m flat-spacing capacitance, 495 MΩ insulation thickness, the unsymmetrical capacitance derivation and the generator P<sub>max</sub> problem, all from older papers and TTs.
2. **TT questions come back in the final.** Seniors wrote "21 TT → Final" on several questions. Your TT questions (string 8/11 kV, Varley loop, P–δ sudden change, unsymmetrical capacitance) are very likely to appear.
3. **Every final had**: 1 unsymmetrical/composite derivation, 1 bundled/telephone maths, 1 cable-stress or loop-test maths, 1 string-efficiency item, 1 sag maths, 1 stability item.
